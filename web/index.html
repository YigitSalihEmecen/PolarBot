System Guide & Documentation

HOW TO CONNECT TO WIFI ▼

When the ESP8266 is turned on without saved credentials, it behaves as an Access Point.

  1. Open your computer or phone's WiFi settings and connect to the network named PolarPlotter-Setup.
  2. A configuration portal should automatically pop up. If it doesn't, navigate to http://192.168.4.1 in your browser.
  3. Select your home WiFi network from the list and enter your password.
  4. The ESP8266 will save these settings permanently and restart.
  5. Ensure your computer is on the same home network, and simply click Connect in this Web App!
HOW TO CALIBRATE BACKLASH ▼

The internal reduction gears inside the 28BYJ-48 stepper motors have microscopic physical gaps between their teeth. This is known as "Backlash". When a motor reverses direction, the internal shaft must turn several degrees before the physical outer gears actually engage and begin pushing the arm.

How to calibrate:

  1. Click the DRAW CALIBRATION TEST button in the Start tab. This draws a 40x40mm square with a crosshair inside.
  2. Use a caliper to measure the physical square drawn on the paper. If the square is 38x38mm, you are missing 2mm of movement due to Arm backlash.
  3. Enter your measured discrepancy into the ARM BACKLASH (MM) box.
  4. If the lines of the crosshair do not perfectly align or the corners of the square are wobbly, adjust the PLATE BACKLASH (DEGREES) slightly (try increments of 0.5 degrees).
  5. Click UPDATE Live Settings and draw the test again until the shape is perfect!
WHAT IS HOMING? ▼

The Polar Plotter uses an absolute coordinate system, but stepper motors are "dumb" and do not know where they are when they power on.

Clicking the Home Arm button forces the arm motor to extend towards the center of the build plate until it hits its physical hard limit. The system then declares this point as X:0 Y:0. The arm also homes automatically every time the plotter powers on. You should always ensure the pen is positioned exactly at the center of the plate before starting a stream.
