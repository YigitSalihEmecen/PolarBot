Guide

Setting up the network, calibrating, and what homing does.

Connecting to WiFi

When the ESP8266 starts without saved credentials, it opens its own access point.

  1. On your computer or phone, join the network PolarPlotter-Setup.
  2. A setup portal should open. If it doesn't, go to http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Pick your home network and enter its password.
  4. The board saves the settings and restarts.
  5. With your computer on the same network, click Connect here.

If polarplotter.local doesn't resolve on your network, replace it with the board's IP address.

Calibrating backlash

The reduction gears inside the 28BYJ-48 motors have small gaps between their teeth. When a motor reverses, the shaft turns a little before the gears engage, so some motion is lost. This is backlash.

  1. Click Draw calibration test in Start. It draws a 40 × 40 mm square with a crosshair.
  2. Measure the square with a caliper. If it is 38 × 38 mm, the arm is losing 2 mm.
  3. Enter that shortfall as the Arm backlash.
  4. If the crosshair lines don't meet or the corners wobble, adjust the Plate backlash in steps of about 0.5°.
  5. Click Send to plotter and draw the test again until the square is right.

Values are not saved on the plotter; it starts with its built-in defaults after every power-on.

What homing does

Stepper motors don't know where they are when they power on. Homing drives the arm toward the centre of the plate until it reaches its mechanical stop, and calls that point X0 Y0.

The arm homes automatically at every power-on. Make sure the pen sits exactly at the centre of the plate before you start a stream.
